A few ground rules

Build boldly.
Play fair.

Everyone should have room to create, learn, and compete on equal terms. Please read these guidelines before registering.

Student teams

Open to currently enrolled college students. Form a team of 2–4; each person may register on one team only.

Build during the event

Start new work after kickoff. You may use existing open-source libraries and tools; disclose them in your submission.

Original work, honest credit

Submit your team's own work. Credit collaborators, datasets, APIs, and third-party assets. Plagiarism means disqualification.

Submit a working demo

Before the deadline, share your project, source code, a short explanation, and a demo or screenshots with the judges.

Judging criteria

Judges consider impact, originality, technical execution, usability, and the clarity of your presentation.

Respect the community

Be kind, inclusive, and respectful. Harassment or disruptive conduct may result in removal from the event.
